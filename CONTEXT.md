# notes.webutvikling.org

Tomas's public notes site. First-person, informal, not to be taken too seriously.

## Language

**Post**:
A dated piece of writing with a title, a publish date, tags, and one Claim.
_Avoid_: Article, note, scribble, blog post, entry

**Claim**:
The single assertion a Post argues. Everything in the Post earns its place by serving this.
_Avoid_: Topic, theme, idea, braindump

**Prerequisite**:
A concept the reader already has before the opening. The Post does not introduce it.
_Avoid_: Audience, background, context

**Link**:
A bookmark with a URL and a short description, and no body of its own.
_Avoid_: Post, article

**Draft**:
A Post that exists in the repo but is hidden from the production site.
_Avoid_: Unpublished, WIP, private

**Archived**:
A Post or Link that exists in the repo but is hidden from both the development and production sites.
_Avoid_: Deleted, hidden, retired

**Category**:
A single classification on a Post or Link: guide, rant, question, scribble, statement, debugging, book, or idea. When omitted, it is a scribble.
_Avoid_: Type, kind

**Tag**:
A label on a Post. A Post has between one and five.
_Avoid_: Category, keyword, topic

**Scribble**:
The default Category — a Post or Link that does not claim to be a guide, rant, or anything else.
_Avoid_: Using this as a name for Posts in general
